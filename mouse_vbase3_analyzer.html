🏛️ 51 Years of Murine Immunogenetics (1975–2026)
Historical Lineage
KM
Georges Köhler & César Milstein (1975)
MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Cambridge • Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1984)

In 1975, Georges Köhler and César Milstein published their landmark paper in Nature describing continuous cultures of fused cells secreting antibody of predefined specificity. By fusing sheep red blood cell-primed BALB/c splenocytes with the P3-X63-Ag8 myeloma line (derived from MOPC-21), they founded hybridoma technology, transforming immunology, molecular biology, and modern biotherapeutics.

RD
Renate Dildrop (1984)
Institute for Genetics, University of Cologne

Renate Dildrop's seminal 1984 classification established the canonical murine VH gene family nomenclature: J558 (VH1, Clan I), 7183 (VH5, Clan III), Q52 (VH2, Clan II), S107 (VH7), 36-60 (VH9), 3609 (VH3), and X24 (VH10). This framework provided the structural taxonomy for all subsequent murine immunoglobulin genetics and remains the gold standard in comparative immunology.

KR
Klaus Rajewsky, Michael Reth & Alfred Bothwell (1981)
University of Cologne & Yale University

The B1-8 murine hybridoma (specific for the (4-hydroxy-3-nitrophenyl)acetyl [NP] hapten) and its V186.2 variable gene provided the experimental foundation for elucidating somatic hypermutation and affinity maturation in B-cells. The single amino acid replacement Trp33Leu (W33L) in CDR1 demonstrated how somatic point mutations drive ten-fold increases in antigen binding affinity during the germinal center reaction.

CW
Marilia Cascalho & Matthias Wabl (1996)
University of California, San Francisco (UCSF)

Created the quasi-monoclonal (QM) mouse from the rearranged 17.2.25 anti-NP heavy chain gene, demonstrating how targeted gene replacement and receptor editing maintain immunological tolerance and diversify primary B-cell repertoires in vivo.

WK
Tai Te Wu, Elvin A. Kabat, Marie-Paule Lefranc & Werner Müller
Kabat Database, IMGT, DNAPLOT & VBASE3

From the pioneering 1970 variability metric and first antibody databases of Wu & Kabat, to the rigorous standardized immunogenetic numbering of Marie-Paule Lefranc (IMGT), and the automated alignment and somatic hypermutation analysis developed by Werner Müller (DNAPLOT) and Ida Retter (VBASE2), VBASE3 stands upon five decades of open comparative immunoinformatics.
