Immunogenomic Coverage & Alignment Release Policy

To guarantee synthesis-grade fidelity and eliminate spurious alignments from draft fragment loci, VBASE3 adheres to an objective Coverage Gating Policy. Dedicated search portals and SIMD alignment workbenches are provided for any vertebrate species that achieves ≥ 80% verified coverage (confirmed functional Class I germlines, physiological AIRR repertoire validation, and strict J-segment boundary compliance). Species currently under 80% remain transparently cataloged as assemblies mature.

⚖️ Objective Coverage Gating Criteria

  • ≥ 80% Coverage (Search Portal Live): High-confidence chromosome-level or deep Sanger locus assembly, verified functional Class I V-genes confirmed with rearranged repertoire reads, multi-locus heavy and light chain representation, and exact IMGT C-terminal J-segment boundaries (TVSS/TVSA for Heavy; VEIK/LEIK for Kappa; TVL for Lambda).
  • < 80% Coverage (Assembly & Curation in Progress): Fragmentary scaffolds, unverified pseudogene arrays, or pending AIRR-seq experimental verification. Kept active in internal research partitions and monitored dynamically until coverage milestones are met.
