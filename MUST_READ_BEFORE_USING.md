## 🔬 Scope of the Initial Beta Release

VBASE3 provides an open, systematic comparative immunogenomic database and high-performance WebAssembly analysis suite for vertebrate antigen receptors:

1. **Class I Master Catalogs (`vbase3_class1_master_catalog.tsv`)**:
   - **2,856 confirmed functional genes** (2,665 V-genes, 41 D-genes, 150 J-genes) across 36 benchmark vertebrate taxa.
   - Every entry is backed by physiological rearranged repertoire evidence ($\ge 75\%$ identity over mature FR1 $	o$ Cys104).
2. **Synthesis-Ready Boundary Invariants**:
   - Mature V-genes terminate strictly at conserved Cys104 (signal peptides cleaved; junctional hypervariable V(D)J CDR3 excluded).
   - J-genes terminate strictly at canonical IMGT boundaries (`TVSS`/`TVSA` for Heavy, `TVL`/`TAL` for Lambda/Rho, `VEIK`/`LEIK`/`VDIK`/`LDIK`/`LELK` for Kappa/Sigma) with zero constant region bleed-through.
3. **Curated TCRBase Germline Repertoires (`tcrbase_germlines_curated.tsv`)**:
   - **3,114 curated TCR genes** across 31 vertebrate species covering TRA, TRB, TRG, and TRD loci.
4. **Rosetta Stone Cross-Reference Table (`vbase3_crossreference_table.tsv`)**:
   - **3,900 cross-mappings** linking IMGT systematic symbols, Retter & Müller VBASE2 IDs, Tomlinson DP names, Kabat records, and INSDC/GenBank accessions.
5. **Modular Constant Region Database (`vbase3_constant_regions.tsv`)**:
   - **108 modular Fc and light chain constant cassettes** with 100% INSDC/GenBank accession provenance.
6. **Client-Side Web Portals (Zero-Backend WebAssembly)**:
   - 26 interactive browser studios running 100% client-side with zero sequence data transmitted to external servers:
     **[https://vbase3.github.io/vbase3_database/](https://vbase3.github.io/vbase3_database/)**

## 📬 How to Submit Corrections & Feedback

We actively solicit corrections, candidate alleles, and feedback from the immunogenomics and antibody engineering communities. If you identify:

* A sequence discrepancy, frame anomaly, or missing allele with published empirical backing
* A nomenclature collision or cross-referencing alignment error
* An edge case in the WebAssembly alignment engine or boundary trimming logic
* Suggestions for the automated continuous harvesting pipeline
