The High-Affinity RabMAb Revolution:

Rabbits (Oryctolagus cuniculus) produce antibodies of exceptional affinity (frequently in the low picomolar to sub-picomolar range) and exquisite specificity. Developed by Katherine Spieker-Polet, Weimin Zhu, and colleagues (Epitomics/Abcam), rabbit monoclonal antibodies (RabMAbs) transformed clinical diagnostic pathology, immunohistochemistry (IHC), and biotherapeutic lead discovery.

Key Structural & Immunogenetic Hallmarks in VBASE3:

  • Inter-CDR Disulfide Bond (Cys21–Cys79): Unlike human and mouse antibodies that possess only the canonical intra-domain Cys23–Cys104 disulfide bond, rabbit heavy chains feature an additional, highly conserved disulfide bridge between Cys21 in FR1 and Cys79 in FR3. This covalent tether stabilizes the variable domain and enables ultra-rigid CDR presentations.
  • Extra Intra-CDR3 Cysteines: Rabbit CDR3 loops frequently contain paired cysteines forming internal disulfide knots that lock extended loop conformations.
  • Somatic Gene Conversion: Rabbits utilize a restricted pool of rearranging VH genes (predominantly IGHV1) which is diversified through massive somatic gene conversion from upstream pseudogenes, followed by extensive somatic hypermutation.
  • Strict Synthesis Invariants: Canonical synthesis endpoints (TVSS for heavy, TVL for lambda, and LEIK for kappa) ensure zero constant region bleed-through in recombinant RabMAb synthesis.
  • 100% Stratum 3 Class I Verified: All 117 rabbit V-genes represent confirmed functional segments with physiological rearranged repertoire evidence, trimmed strictly to Cys104.
