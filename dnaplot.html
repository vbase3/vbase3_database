Nucleic Acids Res. 1997, PMID: 9016537; Lefranc MP, et al. Nucleic Acids Res. 1998, PMID: 9399859). It subsequently served as the core sequence alignment and search page for the automated VBASE2 relational database (Retter I, Althaus HH, Münch R, Müller W. Nucleic Acids Res. 2005, PMID: 15608286; Retter I. PhD Thesis, TU Braunschweig 2006, leopard:dbbs_mods_00013341).
