🏛️ Historical Tribute: The Discovery of Heavy-Chain-Only Antibodies

            


            

                

                    

                        
HC

                        

                            
Cécile Hamers-Casterman & Raymond Hamers (1993)

                            
Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), Belgium

                        

                    

                    

                        In their groundbreaking 1993 Nature discovery ("Naturally occurring antibodies devoid of light chains", Nature 363, 446–448), the Hamers laboratory demonstrated that sera from dromedary camels (*Camelus dromedarius*) and later alpacas and llamas contain functional homodimeric IgG antibodies lacking both light chains and constant CH1 domains. This revolutionized antibody biochemistry.
                    

                


                

                    

                        
SM

                        

                            
Serge Muyldermans (1994–Present)

                            
Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB)

                        

                    

                    

                        Serge Muyldermans pioneered the structural biophysics, crystallization, and immunogenetic classification of single-domain nanobodies. He uncovered the canonical Framework 2 hallmark tetrad substitutions that remodel the hydrophobic VL-interface into a hydrophilic convex surface, preventing aggregation without requiring a light chain partner.
                    

                


                

                    

                        
JS

                        

                            
Jan Steyaert (2007–Present)

                            
VIB-VUB Center for Structural Biology

                        

                    

                    

                        Pioneered the application of nanobodies as crystallization chaperones and conformational biosensors. Steyaert's work enabled atomic-resolution cryo-EM and X-ray crystal structures of elusive GPCRs, ion channels, and dynamic protein complexes that were previously unresolvable using conventional antibodies.
